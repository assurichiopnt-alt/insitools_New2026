On-Site Machining Services & Rentals

On-site machining services and high-performance machine rentals for heavy industry and maintenance operations.

1. Line Boring Machines

On-site line boring service and boring machine rentals for the maintenance of excavators, pump housings, and heavy-equipment bearing bores.

BB5000 Line Boring Machine

Boring diameter: 38.1–609.6 mm

Bar diameter: 57.2 mm; optional 31.8 mm

Max stroke: 914.4 mm

2. Bore Welding Machines

Service and rental of automated bore-welding systems that build up worn surfaces back to standard size before re-boring.

BW1000 Auto Bore Welder

Axial travel: 228.6 mm

Wire diameter: 0.76–1.14 mm

Torch range: 22.4–609.6 mm

3. Flange Facers

On-site flange facing service to fix leaking flange faces in piping systems at refineries, power plants, and drilling rigs.

FF5300 Flange Facer

Facing diameter: 144.8–812.8 mm standard; up to 1016 mm extended

ID mounting diameter: 144.8–909.3 mm

Power: Pneumatic

4. Milling Machines

On-site portable milling service for flat surfacing and slotting of large machine bases and motor mounting platforms. Equipment is selected to suit each project.

5. Key Mills

On-site keyway milling service to repair or cut new keyways on machine shafts without dismantling the components.

KM3000 Key Mill

Keyway length: 152.4 mm

Keyway width: 31.8 mm

Shaft diameter: 19.1–266.7 mm
