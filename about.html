About INSITOOLS

Trusted Industrial Support Backed by 42+ Years of Experience

INSITOOLS is built on the same trusted service values as Assurich Industries. We are committed to delivering reliable on-site machining, welding, and after-sales support to industries that demand performance, safety and continuity.

A Reputation You Can Count On

With more than four decades of industrial equipment distribution and after-sales service, our brand stands for dependable solutions and long-term customer trust across Southeast Asia.

One-Stop Trusted Solutions

We provide one-stop support for on-site machine tools, hydraulic tools, pipe end preparation, engine rebuilding equipment and turnkey project management for marine, offshore, oil & gas, mining and power sectors.

Service and After-Sales Excellence

Our in-house technical and after-sales service teams deliver maintenance, repair and technical support with workshop-level quality directly on-site.

Trusted by industrial teams across multiple sectors.

Our customers rely on us for fast deployment, trusted brands and consistent results. We support manufacturing, steel fabrication, maritime, mining, oil & gas, automotive and food industries with practical on-site engineering.
