BW350 Automated Bore Welder

Automatic bore welders build up worn bores and faces with a controlled weld overlay, so the bore can then be line-bored back to its original size on site.

They are used together with portable boring machines to rebuild worn pin bores and housings without replacing the part.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BW350 Automated Bore Welder used for?

Automatic bore welders build up worn bores and faces with a controlled weld overlay, so the bore can then be line-bored back to its original size on site. They are used together with portable boring machines to rebuild worn pin bores and housings without replacing the part.

INSITOOLS supplies the BW350 Automated Bore Welder from its Singapore headquarters, with service teams supporting customers in Thailand and across ASEAN. On-site machining services and equipment rental are also available – contact the sales team to confirm availability for your project.
