Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PFM165 Mini Flange Facer used for?

Portable flange facing machines re-machine flange sealing faces, gasket grooves and heat-exchanger faces on site, restoring a leak-free seal without removing the pipe, vessel or valve. Typical jobs include raised-face and RTJ flanges, heat-exchanger channel covers, pump and valve flanges.
