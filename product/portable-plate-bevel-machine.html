Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Portable Plate Bevel Machine used for?

Split-frame and clamshell pipe cutters cold-cut and bevel in-line pipe for weld preparation, without heat, sparks or the need to remove the pipe. Typical jobs include pipeline tie-ins, decommissioning and weld preparation in hazardous areas where hot work is restricted.
