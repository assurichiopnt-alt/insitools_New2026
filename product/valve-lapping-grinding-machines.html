Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Valve Lapping/Grinding Machines used for?

In-situ valve grinding and lapping machines restore the sealing seats of gate, globe and safety valves without removing the valve from the line. Typical users are power plants, refineries, petrochemical and marine operators that need to stop seat leakage during short shutdowns.
