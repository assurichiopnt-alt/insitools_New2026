Boring Machines

On-Site Lathe Machine

Portable line boring machines restore worn bores, pin housings and bearing seats in-situ, so heavy equipment, vessels and plant can be repaired where they stand instead of being dismantled and shipped to a workshop.

Typical jobs include excavator and crane pin bores, stern tubes, rudder stocks, hinge points and bearing housings.
