FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is portable on-site machining?

Portable on-site machining uses machines that are carried to the job and mounted directly on the workpiece – such as line boring bars, flange facers and milling machines – so worn bores, flanges and surfaces can be repaired to workshop accuracy without dismantling the equipment.

Why machine on site instead of sending parts to a workshop?

Machining in place avoids dismantling, lifting and transporting large components, so equipment returns to service sooner and plant downtime is reduced. It is often the only practical option for vessels, cranes, heavy plant and in-line piping.

Which machines does INSITOOLS supply?

INSITOOLS supplies portable line boring machines, flange facing machines, gantry and linear milling machines, automatic bore welders, in-situ valve grinding and lapping machines, split-frame pipe cutters and bevelers, and hydraulic power units and torque wrenches.

Can I rent equipment or hire an on-site machining service?

Yes. Besides equipment sales, INSITOOLS offers on-site machining services and equipment rental. Send your job details through the Services & Rentals page and the team will confirm availability.

Which industries and countries does INSITOOLS support?

INSITOOLS supports oil & gas, power and wind energy, marine and shipyards, mining, construction and heavy equipment, steel and metals, pulp and paper, sugar mills, manufacturing and steel fabrication, with headquarters in Singapore and service teams in Thailand and across ASEAN.
